Labeling rules
  • Label the class covering most of the inner yellow square (one 10 m Sentinel-2 pixel), as it was in Jan to Mar 2024. The dashed box is the 3×3 pixel neighbourhood, for context and for imagery offset of a few metres.
  • Pick the capture date closest to early 2024. If the high-res image is older or newer, check the Sentinel-2 chips for change (new buildings, cleared land) and label what the 2024 chips support.
  • Urban: buildings, roads, paved yards, rail, built infrastructure.
  • Vegetation: trees, plantations and orchards (coconut, rubber, mango), grass, parks, shrub and scrub, mangroves, water covered by floating plants.
  • Water: open water: lakes, rivers, backwaters, ponds, reservoirs, sea.
  • Agriculture: cropland with field parcels, standing crops, paddy (also when flooded) and fallow fields that still show the parcel structure.
  • Bare Soil: exposed ground with almost no vegetation: rock, quarries, sand, dry riverbeds, construction sites cleared but not yet built.
  • Mixed pixel with no clear majority: give your best guess and mark Unsure. Use Cannot label only for cloud, missing or blurry imagery.
  • Do not look at the model or Dynamic World maps while labeling. The tool shows their classes only after you label a point; do not go back and change a label because of them. Change a label only if you misjudged the imagery.
